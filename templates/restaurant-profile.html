OUR STORY · ABOUT MOKABO

A place for your everyday escape.

A welcoming corner for everyday breaks, Mokabo Cafe brings together comforting food, good coffee and easy conversation.

Start with an espresso or a creamy cappuccino, pair it with a toasted sandwich, and finish with something sweet. Our sample menu has a little something for every mood, from refreshing cold coffee to a pizza shared with friends.

Imagine warm lighting, relaxed seating and the aroma of freshly brewed coffee: a cozy place to read, reconnect and enjoy the moment.
